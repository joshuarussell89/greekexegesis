Building the diagram

Split
Tap a word to break the line there; the new line is indented one level.
Rejoin
Tap a word in Join mode, press Join ↑, or drag a line's grip up onto the line above.
Indent
Drag a line's grip sideways; it snaps to a 36 pt grid.
Float a word
Drag any single word out of the flow for fine placement.
Auto-split
Breaks at conjunctions, participles, infinitives and relatives — a first draft in one tap.

Annotating

Anchoring
Every mark attaches to its nearest word, so ink follows the text when you move it.
Line
Snaps to true horizontal or vertical within 8°.
Box
Drag corner to corner. Erasing its empty interior is ignored.
Line styles
Tapping Line opens a menu of weights. Thin, regular, dotted and double are ordinary strokes. Extra thick is different: it lays a section break across the page, opens a gap in the flow, and gives you a title to fill in. Tap the same boundary again to remove it.
Colour
Tap the floating puck to open the wheel; drag the hub to move it. 18 colours, shown as tints while the highlighter is active.

Finding and marking

Find
Search the parsing itself. Tap Imperative to light up every imperative in the passage; add Aorist to narrow it to aorist imperatives. Chips combine, and the box accepts a lemma or an English gloss. Accents are ignored, so λυπεω finds λυπέω.
Apply
Highlight, Underline or Box every match at once, in the wheel's current colour.
Mark
Underline a word, box it, or put a small badge above it — ♥, 🩸, ✝, ★ and others. Tap a word to apply, tap again to remove. Marks are attached to the word, so they move with it.

Lexical

Tap a word
Lemma, full parsing, gloss, and a link into Accordance.
Highlight lemma
Colours every occurrence in the passage at once.

Undo

Undo
One history for everything — splits, joins, indents, word moves, ink, boxes, arrows, section breaks and titles. ⌘Z, or the button in the header. ⇧⌘Z redoes.
Scope
Each passage has its own history, and it starts fresh when you switch book or chapter.
