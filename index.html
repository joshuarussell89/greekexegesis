Building the diagram

Split
Tap a word to break the line there; the new line is indented one level.
Rejoin
Tap a word in Join mode, press Join ↑, or drag a line's grip up onto the line above.
Indent
Drag a line's grip sideways; it snaps to a 36 pt grid.
Float a word
Drag any single word out of the flow for fine placement.
Auto-split
Breaks at conjunctions, participles, infinitives and relatives — a first draft in one tap.

Annotating

Anchoring
Every mark attaches to its nearest word, so ink follows the text when you move it.
Line
Snaps to true horizontal or vertical within 8°.
Box
Drag corner to corner. Erasing its empty interior is ignored.
Eraser
Rubs out whatever it passes over — ink, lines, boxes, arrows, and the underline, box or symbol on a word. Lemma highlights are left alone, since those belong to every occurrence; clear those from the word inspector.
Line styles
Tapping Line opens a menu of weights. Thin, regular, dotted and double are ordinary strokes. Extra thick is different: it lays a section break across the page, opens a gap in the flow, and gives you a title to fill in. Tap the same boundary again to remove it.
Colour
Tap the floating puck to open the wheel; drag the hub to move it. 18 colours, shown as tints while the highlighter is active.

Finding and marking

Find
Search the parsing itself. Tap Imperative to light up every imperative in the passage; add Aorist to narrow it to aorist imperatives. Chips combine, and the box accepts a lemma or an English gloss. Accents are ignored, so λυπεω finds λυπέω.
Apply
Highlight, Underline or Box every match at once, in the wheel's current colour.
Mark
Underline a word, box it, or put a small badge above it — ♥, 🩸, ✝, ★ and others. Tap a word to apply, tap again to remove. Marks are attached to the word, so they move with it.

Notes and concordance

Note
Tap a word and type in the Note box. Words carrying a note show a small dot.
Concordance
Every occurrence of that lemma across every book you have loaded, with the verse. Tap a result to go there. Entirely on the device — no connection needed.
Export
⋯ → Export saves the page as an image, or prints it, which on an iPad gives you a PDF via Save to Files.

Scope

Load a full book

Drop GitHub's sblgnt-master.zip here for the whole New Testament — no need to unzip it. Individual -morphgnt.txt files work too, as many at once as you like. Loaded books are remembered; you only do this once.

Symbol generation

Only needed for Ask for one in the symbol picker. Drawing your own needs nothing. The key stays on this device, and whatever comes back is checked as plain path data before it is drawn.

English (ESV)

Free for non-commercial use from api.esv.org. The key stays on this device. Crossway's terms allow a live lookup but not local storage of the text, so the English is fetched when you open the panel and never saved.

Accordance links

Module names must match your own Accordance library. NA28-T includes the critical apparatus; GNT28-T is the same text without it.
